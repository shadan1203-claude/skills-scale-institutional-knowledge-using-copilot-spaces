# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Lead defines quality standards, creates test strategies, and ensures acceptance criteria are validated before release. They collaborate with product and development teams to build quality into the product lifecycle rather than testing it in at the end.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain test plans and test case libraries
- Define Definition of Done (DoD) testing requirements
- Review acceptance criteria for testability before development begins
- Execute manual and coordinate automated test execution
- Identify, document, and triage quality issues
- Validate fixes and perform regression testing
- Participate in release readiness reviews
- Mentor team on quality best practices

### Goals
- Deliver defect-free features to production
- Reduce production incidents through comprehensive testing
- Build a culture of shared quality responsibility

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog grooming sessions
- Daily standups to flag blockers
- Test case reviews with developers
- Release readiness meetings
- Post-incident retrospectives

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers**: Validate test coverage, pair on integration testing, review test plans
- **With Product Managers**: Clarify acceptance criteria and edge cases, validate feature specifications
- **With Project Manager**: Report quality metrics and testing timelines, escalate blockers
- **With DevOps/Ops**: Coordinate test environment setup and production monitoring

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Master / Agile Coach facilitates agile ceremonies, removes impediments, and coaches the team on agile practices and continuous improvement. They serve as a guardian of the process and help the team self-organize to deliver value.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, retrospectives, and reviews
- Identify and help remove blockers and impediments
- Coach team members on agile principles and practices
- Track sprint metrics and team velocity
- Foster psychological safety and transparent communication
- Help resolve conflicts and facilitate problem-solving
- Maintain the sprint backlog and collaborate on capacity planning

### Goals
- Optimize team velocity and delivery cadence
- Create a culture of continuous improvement and learning
- Empower the team to self-organize and make decisions
- Minimize organizational and external impediments

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-ones with team members
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Coaching conversations and feedback sessions

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers**: Remove blockers, coach on agile practices, facilitate ceremonies
- **With Product Managers**: Clarify sprint goals, collaborate on backlog prioritization, ensure capacity alignment
- **With Project Manager**: Provide velocity and burndown data, escalate risks, coordinate sprint schedules
- **With Stakeholders**: Communicate sprint progress, set expectations on delivery cadence

---

## Design Lead / UX Specialist

### Role Summary
Design Lead / UX Specialist collaborates with product and development teams to ensure solutions are user-centric, intuitive, and aligned with brand standards. They advocate for user needs and drive design decisions based on research and usability validation.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Collaborate with product and developers on design trade-offs
- Define and maintain design system and component library
- Review product features for usability and consistency
- Ensure accessibility standards are met and prioritized
- Mentor team on design thinking and user-centered approaches

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Reduce support burden through better usability
- Maintain design consistency across products and platforms
- Maximize user adoption and satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critique sessions with developers
- User research findings and usability testing reports
- Design specifications and component documentation
- Feedback loops with product and stakeholders

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers**: Review designs, discuss implementation feasibility, refine specs based on technical constraints
- **With Product Managers**: Validate design solutions against user needs, align on user flows and requirements
- **With Project Manager**: Communicate design timelines and dependencies, flag design risks
- **With QA/Testing**: Validate design acceptance criteria and accessibility requirements

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Lead / Architect provides technical guidance, designs system architecture, and reviews technical solutions. They balance technical excellence with delivery timelines and help the team navigate complex technical decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Design system architecture and technical approaches for major features
- Review code and technical designs for quality, maintainability, and scalability
- Mentor developers on best practices and technical skills
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Evaluate technology choices and make recommendations
- Participate in capacity planning and effort estimation
- Facilitate technical discussions and design reviews

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and performant solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Build team technical capability and knowledge
- Balance delivery speed with architectural integrity

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review feedback and mentoring sessions
- Technology evaluation reports and recommendations
- Technical risk assessments and mitigation plans

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers**: Provide architectural guidance, review code and designs, mentor on best practices
- **With Product Managers**: Discuss technical feasibility of features, advise on scope and complexity
- **With Project Manager**: Assess technical risks and dependencies, estimate effort for complex work
- **With Operations/DevOps**: Ensure design supports operational requirements and monitoring

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholder / Sponsor provides business context, strategic direction, and approvals for projects. They ensure alignment with organizational priorities and secure necessary resources and support for successful delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success metrics
- Provide strategic context and priority alignment
- Secure funding, resources, and executive support
- Participate in key decision gates (initiation, planning, release)
- Monitor project progress against business goals
- Advocate for the project across the organization
- Resolve organizational blockers and competing priorities

### Goals
- Maximize business value and return on investment
- Ensure strategic alignment with organizational goals
- Enable teams to deliver without organizational friction
- Build organizational capabilities and competitive advantage

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and gate decisions
- Executive status updates and business impact reports
- Stakeholder alignment meetings
- Priority and resource allocation discussions

### Key Interactions
- **With Project Manager**: Monitor progress, provide priority guidance, escalate organizational blockers
- **With Product Managers**: Align on business objectives and success metrics, approve roadmap
- **With Developers**: Participate in key reviews, understand technical feasibility of initiatives
- **With other Stakeholders**: Coordinate cross-functional dependencies and resource allocation

---

## Operations / DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Operations / DevOps Engineer manages deployment pipelines, infrastructure, monitoring, and production support. They enable reliable, scalable, and observable systems and bridge development and operations concerns.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain deployment pipelines and CI/CD infrastructure
- Manage cloud infrastructure, databases, and runtime environments
- Implement monitoring, logging, and alerting systems
- Manage secrets, security compliance, and infrastructure access
- Support incident response and production troubleshooting
- Automate operational tasks and improve operational efficiency
- Document runbooks and operational procedures

### Goals
- Ensure reliable, scalable, and secure production systems
- Minimize deployment friction and time-to-release
- Reduce mean time to detection (MTTD) and mean time to recovery (MTTR)
- Build self-service capabilities for development teams

### Typical Communication
- CI/CD pipeline documentation and best practices
- Incident response and post-mortem discussions
- Infrastructure requirements and capacity planning
- Monitoring dashboards and operational health reports

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers**: Support deployment automation, advise on scalability and operational concerns, troubleshoot production issues
- **With QA/Testing**: Provide test environment infrastructure, coordinate load testing and performance validation
- **With Technical Lead**: Ensure architecture supports operational requirements, plan for scalability and resilience
- **With Project Manager**: Communicate deployment readiness and release windows, report on operational metrics
